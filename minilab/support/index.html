Support

Questions, problems or ideas? Email support.minilab@gmail.com. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version. I usually reply within a few days.

Common questions

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. Enhance and Erase run entirely on your iPhone. See the privacy policy.

Enhance didn't change my photo.

If faces are already sharp, Minilab leaves them alone on purpose, so it never makes a good photo worse. It works best on blurry, old or low-resolution photos.

I don't see "Improve quality" after erasing.

"Improve quality" needs an iPhone with 8 GB of memory (iPhone 15 Pro or newer) running iOS 26 or later. On other iPhones, Erase still works using the fast method.

Why does "Improve quality" ask to download 1.2 GB?

It uses a larger AI model that is downloaded once, from Apple, the first time you use it. Wi-Fi is recommended. After that it works offline.

Why are there ads?

Ads keep Minilab free with no limits and no account. An ad may play while a photo is processing; your first Enhance has no ad.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

Tracking: iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland: Minilab > Settings > Privacy choices.

Something went wrong or the app closed.

Try a smaller photo, close other apps and try again. If it keeps happening, email me with your iPhone model and what you were doing.
