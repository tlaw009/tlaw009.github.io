Minilab Privacy Policy

Effective date: December 15, 2026

Minilab ("the app") is made by Shengxin Luo ("I", "me"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

Your photos stay on your iPhone

Minilab enhances photos and removes objects using AI models that run entirely on your device. Your photos are never uploaded to me or to anyone else. I do not have servers that receive your photos, and I cannot see them.

The app asks for access to your photo library only to open the photos you choose and to save the edited results. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings.

Information I collect

None. Minilab has no accounts, no sign-in and no analytics of its own. I do not collect, store or sell your personal information.

If you choose to share crash reports with app developers in iOS Settings, Apple may send me anonymous crash and performance reports. They do not include your photos.

Advertising

Minilab is free and is paid for by ads. Ads are provided by AppLovin and, through AppLovin, by Google and other advertising partners. To show and measure ads, these companies may collect information such as:

  • device and app information (model, iOS version, language, app version)
  • IP address and an approximate location derived from it
  • ad interactions (which ads were shown or tapped)
  • your device's advertising identifier, only if you allow tracking when iOS asks

These companies handle this information under their own privacy policies:

  • AppLovin Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy

Your choices

  • Tracking: iOS asks whether Minilab may track you. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", your advertising identifier is not shared and you still get ads, just less personalised. You can change this in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in the app under Settings > Privacy choices.
  • US state privacy laws: I do not sell personal information. To opt out of personalised ads, use the tracking setting above.

Downloads from Apple

On supported iPhones, the "Improve quality" feature downloads an extra AI model once, from Apple's servers. This download is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy and contains no information about your photos.

Children

Minilab is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA and UK), and I do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your rights

Because I do not hold personal information about you, there is nothing for me to access, correct or delete. For information collected by advertising partners, contact them through the links above. You can also email me and I will help where I can.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
